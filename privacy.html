Privacy Policy

Transparency & Protection of Your Dental Health Information

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vinayak Dental Care ("we", "our", or "the clinic"), located at B-10 Laxmipooja Complex, Kapadvanj, Gujarat. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and healthcare-related information you entrust with us.

2. What Information We Collect

When you use our website, book an appointment, or submit a message, we may collect the following details:

  • Contact Information: Patient full name, phone number, and optional email address.
  • Appointment Details: Preferred appointment date, preferred time slot, treatment of interest, and dental concerns or medical notes you voluntarily share.
  • System & Consent Data: Digital timestamp of booking, unique reference code (e.g. VDC-4821), and confirmation of consent.

3. Why We Collect Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide you with high-quality dental healthcare services, including:

  • Scheduling and managing your clinic consultations and treatments.
  • Providing you with your appointment status via our online tracker or WhatsApp/SMS/Email notifications.
  • Responding to your dental inquiries and providing preliminary care guidance.
  • Maintaining required clinical records in compliance with dental healthcare regulations.

4. Data Confidentiality & No Third-Party Sales

We never sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information or contact details to third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or lead generators under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures (including SSL encryption, secure servers, and restricted staff access) to protect your records. Personal information is retained only as long as necessary for clinical care and healthcare record-keeping requirements.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to:

  • Inquire about the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections or updates to your contact details.
  • Request the deletion of your online inquiry data, subject to legal medical record requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our clinic directly at info@vinayakdentalcare.com or call +91 99999 00000.
