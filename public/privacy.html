Privacy Policy & Patient Data Notice

Last updated: September 2026 · Vinayak Dental Care, Kapadvanj, Gujarat

1. Introduction & Scope

Vinayak Dental Care ("the Clinic", "we", "us") respects your personal and healthcare privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, book dental visits, or submit contact inquiries, and how we protect and manage that information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to facilitate dental appointments and clinical inquiries:

  • Patient Name (to identify booking requests).
  • Mobile Number (to verify your appointment and coordinate scheduling).
  • Email Address (Optional) (to deliver appointment confirmations and rescheduling updates).
  • Appointment Preferences (preferred treatment, preferred date, and time slot).
  • Clinical Inquiries & Notes (any notes or questions you choose to share with our dental team).

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your information is used strictly for legitimate healthcare and clinic communication purposes:

  • To schedule, confirm, reschedule, or cancel dental visits.
  • To generate anonymous reference codes (e.g. VDC-XXXXXX) for private status tracking.
  • To reply to your questions or medical inquiries.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to third parties for advertising or commercial marketing.

4. Third-Party Processors & Infrastructure

Our platform utilizes trusted, industry-standard infrastructure providers to process data securely:

  • Hosting & Serverless Runtime: Vercel Inc.
  • Encrypted Database: MongoDB Atlas (encrypted in transit and at rest).
  • Email Delivery: Transactional SMTP delivery.
  • Fonts & Maps: Google LLC (Google Fonts and Google Maps embeds).

5. Data Retention & Patient Rights

Appointment and inquiry records are retained in our secure clinical database to maintain operational records and continuity of care. You may request the verification, correction, or deletion of your contact inquiry at any time by contacting our clinic reception.
